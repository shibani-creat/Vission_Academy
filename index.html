About Us

About Vision Academy

Established in 2023, Vision Academy is a premier education and academic support institution dedicated to delivering accessible, structured, and outcome-oriented education across Odisha.


Headquartered in Bhubaneswar, we offer a hybrid learning model combining offline classroom coaching with online education.

Our Mission & Vision

Shaping students for a successful future

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality, affordable education that builds strong academic foundations, confidence, and real-world skills for every student.

Our Vision

To become a leading educational institution known for innovation, excellence, and nurturing future-ready students across the nation.
